New User Guide · Features & Local Storage

What Every Feature Does & How Personal Space Works

New here? Open the interactive walkthrough anytime or browse the quick feature breakdown below.

01. Four Ways to Input a Plant

Drag & drop a photo from your laptop, click Browse Computer, press Ctrl+V anywhere on the page to paste a copied image, paste an https:// image link, or click one of the 4 Sample Plants to test live identification immediately.

02. Plant Part Selector & Privacy

Choose Leaf, Flower, Fruit, or Bark to sharpen Pl@ntNet accuracy—switching parts automatically updates your results. All uploaded photos have GPS & camera EXIF metadata stripped in your browser first.

03. Care Guide & Top 5 Matches

Every identification generates a 4-column Care & Pet Safety Strip (Sunlight, Watering, Cat/Dog Safety, and Environment) plus a Top 5 Possible Matches table—click any row to compare close lookalikes and zoom into reference photos.

04. Zero-Login Personal Garden Space

No email or account needed. With Auto-Save ON, every photo you upload is stored in your browser's local high-capacity IndexedDB vault where you can add personal care notes, mark favorites, and set your plant interests.

05. Direct Desktop Folder Sync

Click Link Desktop Folder (in Chrome, Edge, or Brave) to pick a real folder on your computer. The app automatically saves your plant photos in /photos/*.jpg, care sheets in /care-cards/*.md, and garden-space.json.

06. Universal 1-Click Backup File

Using Firefox, Safari, or moving to a new computer? Click Download Backup File to save your entire photo collection, notes, and interests as a single .json file on your desktop, and restore it anytime via Load Backup File.
