# VxPlants — Instant Plant Identifier & Personal Garden Guide

Welcome to **VxPlants**, a fast, private, and easy-to-use plant identification website built for both **mobile phones** and **desktop computers**. Identify houseplants, flowers, trees, and herbs in seconds, get clear care instructions, and keep your own personal plant journal—with **zero accounts or sign-ups required**.

---

## What You Can Do with VxPlants

### 1. Identify Any Plant in Seconds
Add a plant photo in whichever way is easiest on your device:
- **On Your Phone**: Tap **Take Photo** (or the **Camera** tab in the bottom bar) to snap a picture directly with your phone's camera, or tap **Photo Library** to pick an existing photo.
- **On Your Computer**: Drag and drop an image into the upload box, click **Photo Library** to browse your files, or press **Ctrl+V** (`Cmd+V` on Mac) to paste any copied image from the web.
- **Image Links & Live Samples**: Paste a direct image link (`https://...`), or tap one of the **4 Sample Plants** (*Swiss Cheese Plant*, *Hibiscus*, *Rubber Plant*, or *English Lavender*) to try a live identification right away.

### 2. Instant Care & Pet Safety Guide
Every plant match includes an easy-to-read care summary right at the top:
- **Sunlight**: How much direct or indirect light the plant needs.
- **Watering**: When and how often to water so you avoid overwatering.
- **Pet Safety**: Clear warnings if a plant is known to be toxic or irritating to cats and dogs.
- **Best Spot & Care Tip**: Practical advice on where the plant thrives indoors or outdoors.
- **Compare Lookalikes**: Browse reference photos full-screen and click any row in **Other Possible Matches** to compare similar species side by side.

### 3. Your Private "My Garden" Journal (No Login Needed)
- **Automatic Local Saving**: Every plant you identify can be saved automatically to **My Garden** right in your browser.
- **Personal Care Notes**: Jot down notes on any saved plant card—such as which room it is in, where you found it, or when you last watered it.
- **Favorites & Search**: Filter your collection by *All Plants*, *Favorites*, or *With Notes*, and search by name or note anytime.
- **Easy Backup & Restore**:
  - **Phone & Computer Backup**: Tap **Save Backup** to download your garden collection as a single file, and restore it anytime using **Restore Backup**.
  - **Desktop Folder Sync**: On desktop browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave), click **Sync Desktop Folder** to save your plant photos and care cards directly into a folder on your computer.

## Tips for Getting the Best Plant Match

1. **Focus on a Single Leaf or Flower**: A clear, well-lit close-up of a leaf or flower gives the highest accuracy.
2. **Choose the Plant Part (Optional)**: Selecting *Leaf*, *Flower*, *Fruit*, or *Bark* helps narrow down the match—and switching parts updates your result automatically.
3. **Keep the Background Simple**: Avoid blurry shots or multiple different plants overlapping in the same frame.

## Privacy First & Built for Every Device

- **No Account Required**: Never asks for your email address, password, or personal details.
- **Automatic Location Privacy**: Embedded GPS location data is automatically stripped from your photos before identification.
- **Smooth on All Devices**: Optimized to run smoothly and use minimal memory on both everyday smartphones (including 6 GB RAM devices) and desktop screens.
